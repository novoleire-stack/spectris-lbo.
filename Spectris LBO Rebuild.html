The question

In 2025 KKR and Advent fought for Spectris, a UK precision measurement group. KKR won at £41.75 a share, 105% above the undisturbed price and 20.3x FY2024 EBITDA. I rebuilt the buyout from the offer and financing documents to test what KKR needed to believe to pay that price, and what winning cost.

What I built
  • Divisional operating model (Scientific, Dynamics) from reported results, with a 2025E bridge from H1 actuals
  • Sources and uses with fees built from the published fee letter
  • Debt schedule priced on the commitment letter term sheet
  • Returns, sensitivities, comparables and a bid-by-bid analysis, including both bidders' financing
  • Financing terms review, equity reconciliation and a two-page IC memo
